Account & data controls

Delete your UMC account.

A clear guide to closing your account, removing your personal data, and understanding which healthcare records may remain.

Start here ↓

01 / In the app

Five steps. Always initiated by you.

01

Open the UMC app and sign in to the account you want to delete.

02

Open the side menu and choose Account Management.

03

Patient, doctor, or pharmacy: scroll down and choose Delete Account.

04

Caregiver, diagnostic centre, or clinic assistant: choose Delete Account from the side menu.

05

Read the role-specific notice, choose Delete, and wait for UMC to finish.

Your signed-in session verifies ownership. For your security, support cannot delete an account from a phone call alone.

What applies to every account.

01

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

02

If deletion is blocked, your account remains intact. Finish or cancel active work and settle the displayed amount, then try again.

03

Any caregiver access you hold for another patient is removed, regardless of your main role.

04

Your UMC profile, notification history, local app preferences, and sign-in account are removed, and you are signed out.

05

Completed healthcare and financial records may be retained when they belong to another patient’s medical history or are needed for accounting, settlement, fraud prevention, disputes, or legal obligations.

01

Patient

+

Deletion is blocked while

  • An appointment is awaiting confirmation, confirmed, active, or marked arrived.
  • A pharmacy order is pending, accepted, or active.
  • A diagnostic order is pending, selected, confirmed, payment-pending, active, or arrived.
  • Money is held in escrow, including during a cancellation or refund.
  • Any patient charge shown by UMC remains outstanding.

What is deleted

Your profile, patient group, medicines, dose and reminder history, consents, archived medication data, ABHA link held by UMC, notifications, local alarms, caregiver links, and links from doctors’ active patient rosters.

What may remain

Completed appointments, orders, money fields, service names, status, and timestamps may remain as provider and accounting records. Your personal and clinical details are removed from them. Uploaded diagnostic reports linked to those orders are deleted from UMC storage.

02

Caregiver

+

Deletion conditions

There are no caregiver-specific active-work or dues restrictions. Confirm deletion inside the app.

What is deleted

Your profile, notifications, sign-in account, local app data, caregiver routing records, and your membership in every patient group.

What may remain

The patients you helped keep their medicines, dose history, appointments, orders, tests, and other medical records. Deleting your account does not delete another person’s health record.

03

Doctor

+

Deletion is blocked while

  • Appointments are active, awaiting confirmation, arrived, or have payments or refunds in progress.
  • Penalties or billed commission dues are outstanding.
  • Commission has accrued in the current open cycle when in-person settlement is enabled.

How to resolve it

Complete or cancel appointments, then open Finances to settle penalties or dues. If commission is the only blocker, use Settle & Close. Deletion completes after final settlement is verified.

What is deleted

Your profile, contact details, account, settings, pending requests and invitations, notifications, assistant links, and sign-in access. Patients and caregivers are informed that the account closed.

What may remain

Your name remains on patients you treated, and your medical notes transfer to each patient’s record. Completed appointments, prescriptions, related medical records, and settled financial audit records remain where required.

04

Pharmacy

+

Deletion is blocked while

  • Orders are accepted or active, or money is held in escrow.
  • Open bids await a patient response.
  • Penalties or billed commission dues are outstanding.
  • Commission has accrued in an open in-person settlement cycle.

How to resolve it

Complete or cancel active orders, allow refunds to finish, and withdraw open bids. Open Finances to settle dues. If commission is the only blocker, use Settle & Close.

What is deleted

Your pharmacy profile, active account access, notifications, pending connections, and bid access. Doctors’ preferred-pharmacy links are cleared.

What may remain

Completed orders remain on patients’ records, including the pharmacy name already recorded with the service. Settled finance and accounting audit records may remain.

05

Diagnostic centre

+

Deletion is blocked while

  • A test order is selected, confirmed, active, arrived, or procedure-complete.
  • An order has money held in escrow.
  • Penalties remain outstanding.

How to resolve it

Complete or cancel every active test order, allow payments or refunds to finish, and resolve penalties through Finances.

What is deleted

Your diagnostic-centre profile, active account access, notifications, and local app data.

What may remain

Completed tests and reports remain in the relevant patients’ medical histories. Completed service, payment, and settled financial audit records may also remain.

06

Clinic assistant

+

Deletion conditions

There are no clinic-assistant-specific active-work or dues restrictions. Confirm deletion inside the app.

What is deleted

Your profile, assistant hub, stored name and contact details, doctor links, pending invitations, notifications, sign-in access, and local app data.

What may remain

Doctors and patients keep their own clinic and medical records. Deleting an assistant account does not delete records belonging to a doctor or patient.

03 / Support

If UMC will not let you delete.

  • Read every reason shown in the app; UMC lists all current blockers together.
  • Resolve active work before paying commission, because more commission can accrue while work remains open.
  • If UMC cannot verify dues, check your connection and retry.
  • If an amount looks wrong or you cannot reach the relevant screen, call UMC for guidance.
