Privacy Policy

How Unified Medical Care collects, uses, shares and protects your information, and the rights you have over it.

Unified Medical Care (“UMC”, “the app”) is operated by ASHOKANAND CREATIONS PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated in India under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U26209AP2025PTC120364) (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy covers the UMC mobile app for Android and iOS, the UMC web portal for healthcare providers, and the website unifiedmedicalcare.com.

UMC handles health information. We treat it as sensitive personal data under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules. We do not sell it, rent it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone who is not involved in your care or in running the service.

Contact for any privacy question or request: admin@unifiedmedicalcare.com.

01Who uses UMC

UMC connects the people around one goal: never missing a dose. It serves seven kinds of user, and what we collect depends on the role you hold.

  • Patients take medication and keep their own health record.
  • Caregivers are family members or helpers a patient has invited to see their schedule and be alerted if a dose is missed.
  • Doctors prescribe, consult, and keep clinical records for their patients. Doctors may also use the UMC web portal, which shows the same records under the same rules.
  • Clinic assistants are staff a doctor has authorised to manage appointments and patient details. They cannot prescribe.
  • Pharmacies receive prescriptions and medicine orders and fulfil them.
  • Diagnostic centres receive test orders and upload reports.
  • School infirmaries administer medication to students whose guardians have enrolled them.

Visitors to the website: the marketing site collects nothing beyond the standard requests your browser makes to load it. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

02What we collect

Account and identity

Your name, email address, mobile number and whether it has been verified, how you sign in (Google, Apple or phone), your role, your time zone, and a profile photo if you add one. Patients may also record gender, age, weight and height so that doctors can prescribe safely. Doctors provide their medical registration number, state of registration and specialities, and may draw a signature in the app, which is applied to the prescriptions they issue. Pharmacies and diagnostic centres provide establishment details and licence numbers.

Health information

For patients: medications and schedules; a log of each dose taken or missed, and who marked it; prescriptions; medical history, including diagnosis, clinical notes, reason for visit and follow-up; diagnostic test orders and the reports uploaded against them; recordings and transcripts of consultations you agree to record; and your conversation history with the in-app assistant. If you link an ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account), we store the ABHA number. For doctors and other providers: the clinical records you create for your patients. This is the core function of the app. Without it UMC cannot work.

Photographs and camera

Profile photos, and an optional screenshot when you send us feedback. Doctors may capture a signature image. The camera is also used to scan QR codes and barcodes, for example a prescription code at a pharmacy. Scanning happens on the device and no image is stored. The camera is used only when you open it. We never access it in the background.

Microphone and voice

Three features use the microphone, and each is started by you: recording a consultation (doctor and patient both see that it is on), dictating a voice note, and voice feedback. Consultation audio is uploaded so that it can be transcribed and turned into a structured note. The audio file is deleted once transcription completes, and any that remains is removed within 24 hours. Dictation in English uses your phone’s own speech recognition, and only the text reaches us. Dictation in Telugu or Hindi sends the audio to a speech-to-text provider listed in section 5. We never listen in the background.

Location

Location is used in two ways. First, when you set a home or delivery address, the app can take a one-time precise GPS reading to fill in the address. We store the coordinates with a coarse geohash so that nearby pharmacies and diagnostic centres can be found and distances shown. Second, during an active medicine delivery from a pharmacy, the app shares your live position with that pharmacy so the delivery can reach you. This stops, and the position is removed from the order, when the delivery ends or you stop tracking.

We do not keep a location history, we do not track you at other times, and we never use location for advertising. You can decline location access. You can still type an address by hand, and everything except delivery tracking and distance sorting keeps working.

Contacts

Doctors can save a patient’s name and mobile number to their phone’s contact list, and open a WhatsApp conversation with a patient. When a doctor does this, the app writes that one contact to the phone using the standard Android or iOS permission. We do not read, upload or store your existing contact list. WhatsApp is operated by Meta. Once a conversation opens there, Meta’s own terms apply to it.

Device and notifications

A push-notification token so we can send medication reminders, missed-dose alerts to caregivers, appointment updates, and prescription and order updates. When you submit feedback, the app version and device model are attached so we can reproduce the problem. We do not use crash-reporting, analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not collect an advertising identifier.

Payments

Payments for pharmacy orders, diagnostic tests and consultations are processed by Razorpay. We receive the payment status and a transaction reference. We never see or store your card or bank details. Providers who receive payouts complete identity verification (KYC) with Razorpay, which involves their PAN, legal name, contact details and address.

03Why we collect it

  • To provide the service: reminders, records, prescriptions, orders, and communication between a patient, their care circle and their providers.
  • To authenticate you and keep accounts secure.
  • To send the notifications described above.
  • To transcribe, structure and translate clinical notes with the AI features you choose to use.
  • To process payments and settle providers.
  • To answer your support requests and fix problems you report.
  • To meet legal obligations that apply to health records, payments and companies in India.

We rely on your consent for the sensitive data you give us and for the optional permissions (location, microphone, camera, contacts, notifications), and on our legitimate need to operate, secure and improve the service for the rest. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it to anyone.

04Who can see your data

Your health information is visible only to you and to the people in your care circle: the providers and caregivers that you, or your treating doctor, have connected to your record.

  • A patient sees their own record in full.
  • A caregiver sees the schedules and dose logs of the patients who invited them, and receives missed-dose alerts. Caregivers cannot mark a dose or edit medications.
  • A doctor sees the records of patients under their care.
  • A clinic assistant sees the appointment and patient details of the doctor they work for. They cannot prescribe.
  • A pharmacy sees only the prescription or order sent to it, the patient details needed to dispense and deliver it, and, during a delivery, the patient’s live location.
  • A diagnostic centre sees only the test orders assigned to it and the reports it uploads.
  • A school infirmary sees the medication schedule of enrolled students and logs the doses it administers.

Basic profile information (name and role) is visible to other signed-in users so that, for example, a pharmacy can see which doctor issued a prescription.

Our staff access identifiable health records only when needed to operate or support the service, and each access is limited to the task at hand.

We may disclose information if required by law, a court order or a lawful request from a government authority, and to protect a person’s safety in a genuine emergency.

06ABHA and the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission

If you choose to create or link an ABHA, the app guides you through the National Health Authority’s ABDM flow. The Aadhaar or mobile number and the one-time passwords you enter are sent to ABDM to complete it, and we store the resulting ABHA number on your profile. This is optional. UMC works without it. ABDM’s own privacy policy governs what it does with your data.

07Where data is stored and how it is protected

Your data is stored on Google Cloud in the Mumbai (asia-south1) region of India. Some processing, such as the AI features, sign-in and push delivery, runs on our providers’ infrastructure, which may be outside India. Such transfers are made in accordance with Indian law.

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by the storage provider. Access is restricted by role and by membership in a patient’s care circle, and these limits are enforced on our servers, not only in the app. Adherence logs record who marked each dose, so a record cannot be changed without leaving a trace.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will notify affected users and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

08How long we keep it

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • Health records are kept for as long as the treating provider is required to retain them under Indian medical record-keeping rules, and then deleted or anonymised.
  • Consultation audio is deleted once transcribed, and in any case within 24 hours.
  • Live delivery location is removed when the delivery ends.
  • Feedback is kept until it is resolved and for a limited period afterwards.

09Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data (see section 10).
  • Withdraw consent for optional features such as location, microphone or notifications, at any time, from your phone’s settings or the app.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you are unable to.
  • Raise a grievance with our grievance officer (section 15).

To make a request, email admin@unifiedmedicalcare.com from the email address or mobile number registered on your account. We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Where a record forms part of a medical record that a healthcare provider is legally obliged to retain, we may be unable to delete it before that period expires. We will tell you if that applies.

10Deleting your account

In the app: open the menu, go to Edit Account, and tap Delete Account. Your sign-in is removed on our servers straight away, and your data is handled as described below.

By email: write to admin@unifiedmedicalcare.com with the subject “Delete my account” from the email or mobile number registered on the account. We confirm your identity, complete the deletion within 30 days, and confirm when it is done.

What happens to your data:

  • If you are a patient, your account, profile, medications, schedules, dose logs, assistant chats and uploaded files are deleted. Where a provider served you, your name, address, location and clinical notes are removed from that provider’s copy of the record and any diagnostic report file is deleted. The provider keeps only an anonymised entry with the service, the test name and the amount, so that their accounts still reconcile.
  • If you are a doctor, pharmacy or diagnostic centre, your account and profile are deleted. The clinical records you created for patients remain with those patients, because they are the patient’s medical record, not yours.
  • If you are a caregiver, clinic assistant or infirmary user, your account and profile are deleted and you are removed from every patient record you were linked to.

11Children

UMC is intended for use by adults. Where a patient record belongs to a child, it is created and managed by a parent, legal guardian, the treating healthcare provider, or a school infirmary the guardian has enrolled the child with. We do not knowingly allow children to create their own accounts, and we do not use children’s data for any purpose other than their care.

12Not a medical device

UMC is a record-keeping, reminder and communication tool. It does not diagnose, treat or provide medical advice, and it is not a substitute for professional clinical judgement. Transcripts and structured notes produced by the AI features are drafts for the treating doctor to review. In an emergency, contact a doctor or emergency services directly.
